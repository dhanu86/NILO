# NILO — Your Personal Companion

A little companion to help you stay organized, remember what matters, and make everyday productivity feel effortless.

## About NILO

**NILO** is a personal desktop productivity companion designed to help you manage your daily responsibilities without feeling overwhelmed.

It provides a simple space to organize tasks, keep track of important reminders, and store things you want to remember for later. With a friendly interface and a minimal, professional design, NILO aims to make productivity feel natural rather than stressful.

Unlike a traditional task manager, NILO is envisioned as a personal companion that quietly supports your daily routine.

## Key Features

### 1. Timed Tasks & Smart Reminders
- Create tasks with specific dates and times.
- Receive reminders when a task is due, including when the application is not in the foreground, subject to operating-system permissions and background execution.
- Get notified about overdue tasks when reopening the application after being away or when the computer was switched off.
- Stay aware of upcoming responsibilities without constantly checking your task list.

### 2. Untimed Reference Tasks
- Save tasks, ideas, and things to remember without assigning a deadline.
- Keep useful information accessible for future reference.
- Separate scheduled responsibilities from flexible tasks.

### 3. Task Completion Tracking
- Mark completed tasks with a simple checkmark.
- Clearly distinguish completed tasks from pending ones.
- Keep your task list organized and easy to review.

### 4. Friendly Desktop Experience
- Enjoy a cute yet professional interface.
- Use a compact popup-style experience for quick interactions.
- Experience smooth animations and thoughtful visual feedback.
- Keep the interface simple, distraction-free, and comfortable to use.

### 5. Personal Productivity
- Organize everyday responsibilities in one place.
- Reduce the chances of forgetting important tasks.
- Build a more consistent routine at your own pace.

## Design Philosophy

NILO is built around three ideas:

- **Simple:** Managing a task should take only a few moments.
- **Gentle:** Reminders should help you, not make you feel pressured.
- **Personal:** NILO is a companion for your everyday life, not just another productivity dashboard.

## How It Works

1. **Add a task** — Enter something you need to do or remember.
2. **Choose a schedule** — Set a date and time if the task needs a reminder.
3. **Keep references** — Save tasks that do not require a specific deadline.
4. **Get reminded** — Receive notifications for scheduled responsibilities.
5. **Mark it complete** — Check off tasks as you finish them.
